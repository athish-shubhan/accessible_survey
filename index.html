Before you start

Thank you for helping TransitVic Digital design AccessGo. This survey asks about disruption notifications and alternative journey planning for public transport commuters with vision impairment.

It takes about 10 to 15 minutes. You can answer every question by voice, or by keyboard and touch, whichever works for you. You can switch between the two at any point.

Privacy: if you use voice, your microphone audio is processed by your browser to turn speech into text. Audio is not recorded, saved, or sent anywhere by this site; it exists only for the moment it takes to recognise what you said. Your answers are anonymous and stay in your browser until you choose to submit.

You can say "repeat" to hear a question again, "back" to change a previous answer, "faster" or "slower" to change how quickly I speak, and "pause" to stop listening at any time. Every question also has on-screen buttons and fields if you'd rather not use voice at all.
